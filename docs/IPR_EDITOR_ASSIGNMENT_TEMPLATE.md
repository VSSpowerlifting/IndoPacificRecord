# IPR editor assignment — template

The story-specific half of every editing request. The standing rules live in
`docs/IPR_EDITOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, which is attached to every assignment and
not repeated here. Keep the assignment short: an editor should be able to read
it in two minutes. Fill each bracket, delete lines that do not apply, and send
it from Ben's own account. One manuscript, one assignment, one assigned editor.

For the scheduled Sunday manuscript, the automated email already delivers the
Word and TXT files to Dylan; send this assignment only when there is something
story-specific to add, and use it in full for every additional Brief,
human-drafted manuscript or editor other than Dylan.

---

**Subject:** IPR Brief edit — [slug]

[Name],

Could you edit the attached IPR Brief manuscript? The standing IPR Editor
Instructions are attached; the specifics for this one are below.

- **Working title:** [as in the file]
- **The development, in one sentence:** [what happened, who did it, when]
- **Sources and desks:** [e.g. China MND statements, Japan MOD release; any
  translation, machine-translation or excerpt limits the editor should know]
- **This Brief is:** [the week's regular Brief / an additional Brief this week,
  independent of "[other slug]" — please do not cross-reference the two]
- **Target length:** [700–1,200 words; note if the draft is well outside it
  and what you expect]
- **Due:** [day, date, time ET] — about 48 hours unless stated
- **What I most want from your edit:** [one to three specific asks, e.g.
  "the opening buries the deployment", "section 2 repeats the dek"]
- **Please leave unchanged:** [anything beyond the standing rules, e.g. a
  quotation under translation review, a term of art to keep]
- **Known caveats:** [figures that are the issuer's own, unconfirmed details,
  anything the draft must keep qualified]
- **Credit:** [e.g. "editing credit only" / "we will discuss credit if the
  edit is substantial" — decided by Ben for this issue]

Attached: [slug].docx (your editing file, Track Changes on)[; [slug].txt
(full worksheet, for reference)]; IPR Editor Instructions.

Please reply in this thread with the edited Word file, changes still tracked,
and a short note on what you changed and anything to check. If the timing
does not work, tell me as soon as you can.

Thanks,
Ben

---

### Before sending (owner)

- The Word file opens with the title (Title style), then the dek (Subtitle),
  and ends with the **Source links for context** heading.
- No source IDs, record IDs or worksheet markers appear in the editable text.
- In a week with more than one Brief, each manuscript has its own slug,
  subject line, assignment and editor; returns are reconciled and approved
  separately (`EDITORIAL_QA_CHECKLIST.md`).
- This assignment grants no approval, numbering, publication or credit
  decision beyond what it states.
