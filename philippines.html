How this desk works

Coverage, update rhythm and what IPR adds

Armed Forces of the Philippines
Public-affairs news releases in English. 32 verified publisher links; newest official date 2026-10-18. Collected daily into private custody; verified link metadata is admitted and published automatically once a day.
National Security Council
Official statements in English. Shadow collection only, moving to private custody; no NSC link is published. NSC publication needs its own owner decision; it does not inherit AFP's approval.
Official references vs. IPR analysis
Publisher references on this page are the institutions' own statements, dated by the publisher and not independently verified. IPR analysis appears only in Briefs that an editor has reviewed and the owner has approved; machine screening is never published as analysis.
Briefs from this desk
None published yet. An AFP-sourced dossier separating the Pagsasanay Sanlakas inter-agency exercise from the September 29 JPSCC coordination meeting awaits independent human review; its venue and name-spelling questions remain open.
Not covered
The Department of National Defense, the Philippine Navy, Army, Air Force and Coast Guard as publishers, and all other institutions. Original headlines, article text, photographs and translations are not republished.

Remaining barriers to source admission

Only verified AFP official-link metadata is authorized for publication.
On October 18, 2026 the owner authorized automatic publication of verified neutral AFP link metadata (official URL, publisher date and attributed issuer) without per-record review or an elapsed-day waiting period. Links after the October 15 reviewed cohort are not individually reviewed, and changed or disputed sources are held. AFP titles, bodies, images and translations, and admission to the IPR corpus, still need their own source, rights and editorial review. NSC must qualify separately.
Publisher-specific rights and canonical evidence custody remain unresolved.
Review the authorization for each intended use—private preservation, public Git, public title/link reference, excerpt or full body—before publication. The private reader is not a source-use license.
Two institutional sources are not countrywide coverage.
The Department of National Defense, most Philippine military releases and all other institutions are outside this initial source scope.

Limits of this desk, stated plainly

  • No Philippine source is enabled in the public IPR corpus; any AFP links listed here are verified official-link metadata only (URL, date, publisher), and private AFP and NSC shadow states remain separate.
  • No original body, binary capture or shadow archive record becomes public by this desk declaration.
  • AFP and NSC are independently governed institutional sources, not comprehensive Philippine defense coverage.
