# Content and data rights

This repository holds several different kinds of material, and they are **not**
under the same terms. One licence file covering all of them would be wrong in
both directions: it would give away rights that are not this project's to give,
and it would claim rights over public facts that nobody owns.

This document says which is which. Section 7 is the canonical operating
source-use standard for both IPR repositories; source-specific decisions retain
their actual scope. It does not license third-party material to downstream users.

## 3. Official source documents — the originating institution's terms

Official source documents, quotations, images and other third-party material
collected or referenced by this project remain subject to applicable law,
rights, licences and binding terms. Government publication or public access
alone does not establish public-domain status or unrestricted reuse.

This project does not acquire, transfer or sublicense any right in that
material. It links to and uses official publications within an established
use-specific basis (§7); it does not relicense them. Anyone reusing a collected
document must establish their own applicable basis and comply with its conditions.

**Nothing here is a claim that government or ministry material is owned by this
project.** It is not.

**Map base.** The Desks-page map is drawn from Natural Earth country
boundaries, which are in the public domain. The generalized geometry derived
from them is part of the software under §1; it carries no boundary claim.

## 4. Underlying facts — owned by nobody

Indo-Pacific Record claims no ownership over the underlying public facts: that
an exercise occurred, that a vessel transited, that an official said a thing on
a date. Facts are not copyrightable and this project does not pretend otherwise.

What is protectable is the expression — the selection, arrangement, translation
choices and written analysis — not the events.

## 5. Corpus metadata and provenance

Corpus metadata and provenance records — canonical URLs, publication dates,
issuing institution, content hashes, collection-run evidence and extraction
status — are provided for **research, verification and reference**.

They exist so a reader can check the record rather than trust it. Reuse for
verification, citation and research is the purpose they were built for.

Note that metadata describing a document is not the document. Metadata being
available for reference does not carry any right to the underlying official
text, which stays under §3.

## 6. What the MIT licence does not silently do

Stated plainly, because this is the confusion the document exists to prevent:

* it does **not** relicense collected official texts (§3)
* it does **not** relicense original editorial publications (§2)
* it does **not** transfer rights in third-party images or quotations (§3)
* it does **not** convert the corpus into a public-domain dataset (§3, §5)

The MIT grant applies to the software in this repository. Nothing more.

## 7. Research-first source-use standard

**Founder decision, 4 November 2026; implementation candidate for founder
merge review.** Pursue the maximum defensible research capability consistent
with applicable law, binding publisher terms, truthful provenance and
source-specific safeguards, using the least burdensome effective governance.
Written publisher permission is **one possible basis, not a universal
prerequisite**. This changes the general evaluation rule, not any source's
effective authorization, hold, configuration or historical-content disposition.

### 7.1 Establish a basis for the particular activity

Investigate public-domain status, an applicable open licence, actual
rights-holder permission, an applicable statutory exception or limitation
(including fair use where applicable), or another demonstrated legal
authorization. These are alternatives with different conditions, not
interchangeable approvals. A publisher's ownership notice is evidence of a
claim, not by itself a determination of the reach of statutory exceptions.
Conversely, absence of a prohibition, an accessible URL, attribution, private
storage, IPR's research purpose, size or perceived enforcement risk establishes
none of those bases.

Use the existing source decision/issue/PR and the source definition's policy
reference. Identify the publisher and actual author/issuer, exact work or
standing family scope, intended activity and audience, relevant jurisdiction,
primary legal/publisher evidence and version/date, conditions and exclusions,
and the reason the basis covers that use. Record limits and unresolved material
questions there. Do not create a new permission form, approval dossier or
per-record review queue. A standing determination may cover routine eligible
records; source/version or terms changes and genuine exceptions receive the
existing hold/review treatment.

Classify the **activity**, not the country, as supported, restricted,
unresolved or separately decision-dependent. Supported means evidence supports
the stated use; it does not switch on collection, processing or publication.
An owner signature authenticates a decision, not a legal conclusion. An enum,
manifest, successful request or machine-generated explanation proves no rights.

### 7.2 Keep activities separate

| Intended activity | Required distinction and practical boundary |
|---|---|
| Discover and link | Evaluate the permitted discovery method and any applicable linking restrictions. Prefer an official outbound link and factual identification; a link grant does not cover copied titles, bodies or attachments. |
| Retrieve | Respect robots, actual access rules, rate limits and binding terms. Do not bypass challenges, paywalls, 403s or other controls, impersonate a browser or retry through a different route to evade a refusal. |
| Retain an authentic original | Establish retention scope and custody/access conditions independently. Preserve immutable versions, hashes and recoverability within authorized custody; a private repository is still a copy. |
| Extract/index facts or metadata | Separate facts from protected wording, selection and arrangement, database rights and contractual extraction limits. Do not reproduce expression merely by calling it metadata. |
| Send material to external AI | Assess the actual full/partial input, provider, purpose, storage/training/retention terms, jurisdiction and transfers. Permission for custody or an independent final summary does not establish input authority. A training exception does not automatically cover inference or provider transmission. |
| Write independent source-grounded analysis | Prefer independently worded factual research with citations and clear inference boundaries. Inspect for copied expression or substitution for the original; the label “analysis” or “AI summary” establishes no exception. Human Briefs retain editorial approval. |
| Quote or translate expression | Establish scope for the chosen passage and use. Translation can reproduce protected expression; a full translation is not automatically independent analysis. Preserve attribution, context and any licence conditions. |
| Reproduce originals/substantial excerpts | Require a basis covering that extent and distribution. If unsupported, use permitted links, independent research or bounded quotation instead. Neither complete private custody nor a quotation limit authorizes a public mirror. |
| Display media/attachments | Check each photograph, chart, PDF, HWPX, annex and embedded third-party item; website or text terms may exclude them. No inherited clearance from the surrounding release. |
| Expose through DB/Git/download/API/feed/search/HTML | All are distribution surfaces. A hidden HTML panel or noindex page does not make public Git/SQLite private. Apply the same permitted-component projection to every public surface. |

### 7.3 Apply law and terms without inventing clearance

For U.S. uses, start with [17 U.S.C. §§101, 105 and 107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html).
Section 105's U.S.-government-work rule requires actual qualifying authorship
and official duties; inspect its exceptions. Hosting by a federal institution
does not establish that contractor work, transferred copyrights, photographs
or third-party material qualify. Foreign governments are not U.S. government
authors under that rule, and U.S. status does not resolve rights abroad.

For a proposed U.S. fair use, assess all four factors: purpose/character,
nature of the work, amount/substantiality, and market effect. Explain the
actual use and contrary considerations, including potential substitution and
commercial/provider involvement. Research and nonprofit character are relevant,
not dispositive. There is no universal word-count or percentage safe harbor.
The [U.S. Copyright Office's guidance and case index](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/)
are starting points, not determinations for IPR's particular use. An IPR
operational quotation cap is a limit, not a legal safe harbor.

Identify the law relevant to the acts and distribution in question, not simply
the publisher's country or IPR's location. Foreign exceptions, government-work
rules, moral/database rights and contractual conditions differ. Use current
original-language primary sources; translations assist interpretation and may
lag amendments. Relevant starting points, **not source-family clearances**, are:

| Jurisdiction | Primary material and issue to evaluate |
|---|---|
| China | [NCAC copyright law, 2020 amendment](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/flfg_532/202103/t20210309_50530.html), especially Articles 5 and 24; distinguish excluded official texts/facts from protected newspaper or agency expression and the conditions on exceptions. |
| Singapore | [Copyright Act 2021](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CA2021) and [IPOS guidance](https://ask.gov.sg/ipos/questions/clglxzrrv00f5l408in5a0ozr); fair use and computational-analysis provisions have distinct conditions. They do not displace IPR's specific MINDEF written-grant decision. |
| Korea | [Copyright Act, official legal-information translation](https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_mobile/viewer.do?hseq=71274&key=Copyright&type=lawname), including public-work and exception provisions; [Policy Briefing copyright policy](https://www.korea.kr/guide/copyRight.do) and actual item-level KOGL category/exclusions remain essential. |
| Japan | [Copyright Act, e-Gov](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/345AC0000000048) and [MOD reuse notice](https://www.mod.go.jp/j/info/contents.html); assess the actual PDL scope, third-party exclusions and the separate conditions of quotation/other exceptions. MOD evidence cannot clear JCG. |
| Vietnam | [Official 67/VBHN-VPQH consolidation, 23 March 2026](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/van-ban-hop-nhat-so-67-vbhn-vpqh-469197.htm); the [existing VPCP assessment](docs/engineering/VIETNAM_GOVERNMENT_DOCUMENT_TEXT_2026-10-23.md) distinguishes administrative primary documents from attachments and conditional AI-related provisions. It is not a general MPS or foreign-source exemption. |
| Philippines | [RA 8293, Supreme Court E-Library](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4371), §§175–176 and 185: government authorship, exploitation for profit, assigned/third-party rights and fair use require separate attention; verify applicable amendments and item identity. |
| Indonesia | [Law 28/2014, official DGIP register](https://jdih.dgip.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/346/t/undangundang%2Brepublik%2Bindonesia%2Bnomor%2B28%2Btahun%2B2014%2Btentang%2Bhak%2Bcipta); distinguish official instruments, government-published works, exceptions and third-party content rather than importing §105. |

Keep copyright, binding contract, access, privacy, confidentiality, security,
quality and editorial restrictions separate. Do not assume a footer overrides
statute or that a copyright exception defeats an enforceable contract. If
applicability/enforceability is materially disputed, record the competing
interpretations and consequence in the existing decision: for example, an
exception might support limited commentary while binding terms still prevent
automated retrieval or provider transmission. Hold the affected unresolved
activity; continue other genuinely supported activities within existing authority.
Qualified legal review is appropriate for a material unresolved conflict,
cross-border/provider transfer or proposed substantial republication; it is
not a universal launch prerequisite. No publisher outreach is implied.

### 7.4 Existing controls and decisions remain effective

The [eight-desk source account](docs/desks/EIGHT_DESK_OPERATIONAL_COMPLETION_PLAN.md#61-source-use-reconciliation--4-november-2026)
identifies supported scopes, restrictions and unresolved decisions. Source
policies/manifests and actual grants or assessments control exact activities;
the [shared desk standard](docs/desks/README.md) controls implementation.
Retire only the general insistence on publisher writing where another basis
has actually been established. Preserve MINDEF's specific narrow written-grant
requirement, KOGL/PDL exclusions, China decision-dependent controls, real QA and
recovery holds, access restrictions, paid-output/attempt history and budgets.

This reform does not relax a current hold, enable an additional source, widen
allowed fields, sign a source decision or supply external commercial-model
authority. A newly supported basis for a previously held use must first be
reconciled with its existing source-specific decision and enforcement path;
substantive rights/governance merge, activation, spending, release and
historical-content changes retain their separately applicable founder authority.
Routine low-risk work already covered by established standing authority and
quality controls needs neither a new publisher request nor per-record owner
sign-off. Unresolved or prohibited uses remain held. Preserve historical
decisions and evidence; supersede active general rules explicitly, never
rewrite history or remove public historical material under this mandate.
