This is a commemorative political-work piece published by official military media retelling the story of Huang Jiguang, the Korean War soldier from the 6th Company, 2nd Battalion, 135th Regiment, 45th Division, 15th Army who died blocking an enemy bunker at Triangle Hill (上甘岭) on October 19, 1952, and documenting the living traditions of the unit now designated the 'Huang Jiguang Hero Company' within China's airborne forces. The article's analytical value is as a record of how the PLA frames heroic sacrifice for a junior-officer and youth audience — emphasizing personal vulnerability alongside resolve, and explicitly extending the 'battlefield' metaphor to civilian life through the concept of 担当 (responsibility/commitment). It documents the roll-call tradition and preserved bunk as institutionalized memory practices, but provides no information about the unit's current posture, readiness, or operational role.
