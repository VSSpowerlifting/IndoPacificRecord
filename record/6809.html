Summary

At the MND's November 26 routine press conference, Spokesperson Senior Colonel Chen Xi called on Japan to accelerate destruction of its abandoned chemical weapons (ACWs) in China, tying the demand to the concurrent screening of the documentary Return Our Clean Land at the Conference of the States Parties to the Chemical Weapons Convention and to the 80th anniversary of the Tokyo Trial. Chen's statement frames Japan's ACW obligations as a live political and legal liability rather than a settled historical matter, invoking the phrase 军国主义遗毒 ('poisonous legacy of militarism') to connect the disposal dispute to broader Chinese messaging on Japanese wartime accountability. The framing suggests Beijing is using the CWC multilateral venue to apply additional diplomatic pressure on Tokyo, though the press conference itself provides no new information on the status of specific burial sites or the pace of ongoing destruction operations.

国防部督促日方全面加快日遗化武处置进程 MND Urges the Japanese Side to Accelerate the Disposal Process of Japan's Abandoned Chemical Weapons 11月26日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校答记者问。 记者：11月23日，中方发布了《还我净土》日遗化武纪实片，并在《禁止化学武器公约》缔约国大会期间同步播放。有分析认为，正是日方以各种理由拖延销毁进度，致使大量日遗化武至今未被处置。请问对此有何评论？ Question:On November 23, China released a documentary titled Return Our Clean Land on Japanese Abandoned Chemical Weapons (ACWs), which was screened concurrently during the Conference of the States Parties to the Chemical Weapons Convention. Some analysts point out that it is because Japan has delayed the destruction process under various excuses that a large quantity of its ACWs are left undisposed of to this day. What is your comment? 陈曦：今年是东京审判开庭80周年，也是《禁止化学武器公约》生效29周年。二战期间，侵华日军研制使用化学武器，对中国人民犯下罄竹难书的反人类罪行。《还我净土》日遗化武纪实片用丰富的历史资料、大量的证人证言、日方人员的反思忏悔，还原日本侵略者丧心病狂的犯罪行为，反映日遗化武的深远毒害，讲明全面彻底销毁日遗化武，是日方不容推卸的历史、政治和法律责任。 Chen Xi:2026 marks the 80th year of the opening of the Tokyo Trial and the 29th year of the entry into force of the Chemical Weapons Convention. During World War II, the invading Japanese forces developed and used chemical weapons against the Chinese people, committing untold crimes against humanity. The documentary Return Our Clean Land draws on extensive historical materials, witness testimonies, and reflections and confessions made by Japanese personnel. It recreates the heinous crimes of Japanese aggressors, reveals the lasting harm caused by Japan's ACWs, and underlines that the complete and thorough destruction of Japanese ACWs is a historical, political and legal responsibility that Japan must not shirk. 日本政府在日遗化武问题上长期消极敷衍，以各种借口拖延销毁进度，其“尽快彻底销毁”的政治承诺就要沦为“空头支票”了。不仅如此，日本右翼势力违背国际公理和人类良知，公然抛出所谓日遗化武“自然无害”“责任追诉时效已过”等谬论。若日方连当年侵略中国时遗留下的化武都不愿清理干净，又怎能彻底清算军国主义遗毒？ The Japanese government has long been passive and perfunctory on the issue of its ACWs, delaying the destruction process under various pretexts. Its political commitment to "complete and thorough destruction at an early date" risks becoming an empty promise. Worse still, Japanese right-wing forces, in defiance of international justice and human conscience, blatantly claim that the ACWs have gradually become "not harmful" and that "the legal statutes of limitations for liability have passed". If Japan is unwilling to clean up the chemical weapons left behind during its aggression against China, how can it thoroughly root out the legacy of militarism? 历史罪责不容逃避，责任义务不可推脱。我们督促日方尽快提供有效埋藏线索信息，加大人力资源投入，全面加快日遗化武处置进程，早日彻底清除日遗化武毒害，还中国人民以净土。 Historical crimes must not be evaded, and responsibilities and obligations must not be shunned. We urge the Japanese side to provide effective information on burial sites as soon as possible, invest more human resources, and accelerate the disposal process, so that the day will come sooner when the hazardous Japanese ACWs are destroyed completely and thoroughly, and the Chinese people can live on a clean land.
