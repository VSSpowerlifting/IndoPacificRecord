The CMC Political Work Department, the CPC Central Committee's Party History and Literature Research Institute, the National Radio and Television Administration, and China Media Group have jointly produced a seven-episode documentary, 《伟大远征》 (The Great Expedition), airing on CCTV-1 in primetime October 15–21, with simulcast on CCTV-7 and the Xuexi Qiangguo platform. The framing points to a persistent institutional effort to ground contemporary discipline norms in revolutionary precedent, but the article documents messaging, not a change in policy or posture.
