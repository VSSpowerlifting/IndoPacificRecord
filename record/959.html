At a joint-training demonstration on June 1 for the China-Mongolia 'Steppe Partners-2026' exercise, PLA and Mongolian forces showcased unmanned ground vehicles (robotic 'wolves' for fire suppression and bounding overwatch), FPV drones for obstacle breaching, a 200 kg-payload casualty evacuation UGV, heavy-lift UAVs for precision logistics delivery, and mechanical exoskeletons increasing individual load capacity by 30–40 kg. The demonstration documents the PLA's current integration of unmanned and human-augmentation systems into combined-arms maneuver tasks — fire support, breach, CASEVAC, and field repair — at the small-unit level.
