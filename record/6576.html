SHENZHEN, Nov. 18 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping and his wife, Peng Liyuan, on Wednesday hosted a banquet in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, to welcome guests who are here to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting. "I wish to extend, on behalf of the Chinese government and the Chinese people, our warmest welcome to all distinguished guests," Xi said in his toast. Openness is deeply rooted in Shenzhen's DNA and innovation drives the city's vitality, while cooperation is where its strength comes from, Xi said, adding that the success of Shenzhen is also a microcosm of China's reform and opening up, as well as an example of fruitful cooperation across the Asia-Pacific. Noting that openness, innovation and cooperation have been the distinctive features of APEC since its establishment, Xi said APEC has grasped the pulse of the times and guided the process of regional economic integration, making the Asia-Pacific the most dynamic region and a vital growth engine of the world economy. "At tomorrow's Leaders' Meeting, we will discuss steps to expand mutual openness, explore pathways to innovation-driven growth, extend opportunities for result-oriented cooperation across various fields," Xi said. Before the banquet, Xi and Peng posed for group photos with leaders and representatives from APEC economies and their spouses. After the banquet, they joined the guests to watch an artistic performance.
