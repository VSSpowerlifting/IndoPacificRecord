Three kinds of text, kept apart

How this publication works
Source record
What an institution published: its title, its text where that can be shown, the date it gave and a link to the original.
Machine output
Translations and summaries made by a model. They are published without human review and can be wrong.
Human-controlled analysis
Briefs and dossiers. People make the judgments, and each one shows the sources it rests on.
