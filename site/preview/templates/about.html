What this project does

{{ title }} preserves official defense and security texts, with translations where available, and records what collection found, missed, or could not retrieve. Collection gaps are published because an archive with invisible gaps cannot support responsible comparison.

Founded in 2026 with a focus on official Chinese military and security publications, the project now organizes its records by geographic desk. The China Desk's original record remains available alongside newer regional coverage. Additional desks appear only after their sources are documented and collection reliability is established.

This is not comprehensive Indo-Pacific monitoring. It is a record of what a declared, published, checkable set of institutions said, with the gaps in that record shown rather than smoothed. Institutional authority is a claim about position, not truthfulness: this project records what an institution declared, preserves the source, and keeps that record separate from any interpretation of it.

The project analyzes public institutional discourse. Official statements show what an institution said; they do not by themselves establish policy, capability, intent, or whether a described event occurred. Coverage is selective.
