What it is

{{ title }} preserves official defense and security texts, with translations where available, and records what collection found, missed, or could not retrieve. Collection gaps are published because an archive with invisible gaps cannot support responsible comparison.

This is not comprehensive Indo-Pacific monitoring. It is a record of what a declared, checkable set of institutions said; Coverage shows which.

An official statement shows what an institution said. It does not by itself establish policy, capability, intent, or whether a described event occurred. The record is kept apart from any interpretation of it; Methodology sets out how.

Who publishes it

{{ title }} was founded by {{ maintainer.name }}. Research, editorial review and communications are supported by its editorial team. It is an independent research project.

Founded in 2026 with a focus on official Chinese military and security publications, the project now organizes its records by geographic desk. The China Desk's original record remains available alongside newer regional coverage. Additional desks appear only after their sources are documented and collection reliability is established.

Official source material
Texts, quotations, images and attachments stay subject to whatever law, licence or terms apply to them. IPR grants no right to reuse them. Publication by a government, or on a public web page, does not by itself make material free to reuse, so check the source first.

Facts
Facts, such as that an exercise took place or that an official spoke on a given date, belong to no one.
Record details
Addresses, dates, institutions, content hashes and collection evidence are for research, verification and citation. They carry no right to the text they describe.
