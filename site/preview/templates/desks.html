    What a state means

    What each desk state means, and what it implies about the record.
    StateMeaning
    Live — collecting The desk's sources are in the collection schedule and what they publish is added to the record. Only these desks have record counts.
    Shadow evaluation — not in corpus Trial collection. Source material is being evaluated separately and is not part of the public record or its counts. The desk's page may still set out its scope or list approved official-source links; those links are not IPR records.
    Access blocked — not collecting Researched and reachable in principle, but the institution's site does not admit an automated reader that identifies itself honestly. Nothing has been collected.
    Researched — not yet collecting The desk's sources are documented and none is in the collection schedule. Nothing is collected; where the software to collect a source exists, it has not been started.
    Planned — nothing collected The scope is declared. Nothing has been researched, set up or collected.
    Paused — collection stopped Set up and deliberately stopped. Its existing records stay in the record; nothing new arrives.
    Research published IPR has published its own research on this desk. It is shown beside the desk's collection state, never in place of it, and says nothing about how many records the desk holds.

    Only collecting desks show record totals. A desk in trial collection may hold originals privately; that material is left out of every total and date on this page, which does not mean none was collected.
