## 1. Editorial decision

The lead is the 28 October agreement by Japan's defense minister and the U.S. Secretary of War to establish an AMRAAM final assembly and check-out (FACO) facility in Japan, together with an agreement to share technical information for SM-3 Block IIA production.

**Why this week.** In April the allies put circuit-card assembly into the AMRAAM co-production scope and said they would keep discussing "the possibility" of FACO in Japan. On 28 October both language versions of the ministry summary record an *agreement* to establish the facility. That is the week's increment. Neither summary establishes a site, operator, contract, schedule, output target, built facility or increased production.

**Secondary item.** The same-day Japan–Philippines joint statement on Abukuma-class destroyers is kept as a separate section. It is a stated aim built on a 31 May consensus. It is not a transfer, and the published summaries do not connect it to the missile decisions.

## 4. Discrepancies found and how they were resolved

1. **"Cebu" was unsupported.** The earlier draft placed the U.S.–Japan meeting in Cebu. s1 says only that the ministers met in the Philippines during ADMM-Plus. Corrected to "in the Philippines."
2. **Mitsubishi Electric's role.** The earlier wording implied the company had no contracts or was negotiating to be operator. s3 says it studied participation under MOD contracts awarded in 2024 and 2025 and describes FACO only as a future possibility. P4 now says that, and ends: "It did not present itself as the future assembler."
3. **The 31 May baseline was overstated.** The prior docket called it a "pre-existing broad agreement to pursue transfers." s6 records a broad consensus *to proceed with discussions* toward transfers. P9 and this docket now use that wording.
4. **English and Japanese Philippines texts differ in tone.** s5 (English) says the authorities "concurred in aiming" to transfer the first ship. s7 (Japanese) frames it as an aim on which consultations continue (協議を継続). P8 reports both.
5. **ACSA entry-into-force month conflicts.** s5 (English) says July; s7 (Japanese) says 本年8月 (August). The article does not use this date. Recorded in the s7 locator for Dylan and the owner.
6. **DICAS naming.** "Renamed" was imprecise; s2 calls the meeting DICAS 2.0 as an upgrade of the forum. P3 now says the meeting "upgraded the forum to 'DICAS 2.0.'"
7. **P6 citation.** P6 previously cited s3 for April's component answer. That claim is in s2; s3 was removed from P6's support.
8. **Over-reach in the old docket.** The previous version said every page was "checked directly on publisher site" with repeated boilerplate. This docket replaces those lines with the actual method above.
