What each desk reads

China Desk Live — collecting

Chinese official and state-linked defense publication surfaces: the armed forces newspaper, the defense ministry, the state news agency's military desk, and two English-language portals.

Browse its records

Singapore Desk Live — collecting

One publication family: official releases from the Singapore Ministry of Defence, which publishes for itself and for the Singapore Armed Forces.

Browse its records

Japan Desk Shadow evaluation — not in corpus

Official Japanese-language Ministry of Defense RSS discovery from its news and site-update streams, plus a separate Japan Coast Guard English press-release shadow pilot. Neither covers all Japanese defense or maritime-security publications, and neither MOD feed represents Joint Staff or English MOD releases.

South Korea Desk Live — collecting

Source-verified MND and DAPA official-link metadata from Korea Policy Briefing; separately licensed Korean government original text; machine-translated MND releases, labeled as unreviewed machine output; and editor-approved English translation and analysis. Coverage is selective; government claims remain attributed to their issuing institutions.

MND: 49 verified links. Links to MND and DAPA pages are listed; see the Korea Desk for DAPA links and reuse limits. This is not full coverage of the country.

Browse its records

Philippines Desk Official links and machine explanations — not in corpus

Bounded first-party Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) public-affairs publications and Philippine National Security Council (NSC) official statements. Not a comprehensive defense, military, foreign-policy or whole-of-government archive.

51 verified official links and 7 machine explanations. AFP and NSC links and attributed machine explanations are published automatically; original documents are not in the public record. These are separate from records, which stay at none.

Vietnam Desk Live — collecting Research published

Public Security foreign-affairs releases and eligible Government Office administrative documents use separate source-use decisions and the shared Daily. Bibliographic references remain. Other source families are restricted or inactive; coverage is selective. Government News is a tag that editors apply, not a complete defense category.

Browse its records

Indonesia Desk Shadow evaluation — not in corpus

A bounded institutional-news research desk focused initially on Kementerian Pertahanan Republik Indonesia (Kemhan) Berita, in original Indonesian. Coverage excludes the armed forces' wider publications, ministerial decrees, and national defense-policy documents.

US Indo-Pacific Reference Desk Access blocked — not collecting

Declared as a reference desk, deliberately bounded: the minimum US institutional output needed to read other desks' framing against a US framing of the same events. It is not a feed of US defense releases.

What a state means

What each desk state means, and what it implies about the record.
StateMeaning
Live — collecting The desk's sources are in the collection schedule and what they publish is added to the record. Only these desks have record counts.
Shadow evaluation — not in corpus Trial collection. Source material is being evaluated separately and is not part of the public record or its counts. The desk's page may still set out its scope or list approved official-source links; those links are not IPR records.
Access blocked — not collecting Researched and reachable in principle, but the institution's site does not admit an automated reader that identifies itself honestly. Nothing has been collected.
Researched — not yet collecting The desk's sources are documented and none is in the collection schedule. Nothing is collected; where the software to collect a source exists, it has not been started.
Planned — nothing collected The scope is declared. Nothing has been researched, set up or collected.
Paused — collection stopped Set up and deliberately stopped. Its existing records stay in the record; nothing new arrives.
Research published IPR has published its own research on this desk. It is shown beside the desk's collection state, never in place of it, and says nothing about how many records the desk holds.

Only collecting desks show record totals. A desk in trial collection may hold originals privately; that material is left out of every total and date on this page, which does not mean none was collected.
