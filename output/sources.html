Reading the list

Authority describes position, not truthfulness

The letter says how close a source sits to the institution that speaks. An A source is closer. It is not more likely to be accurate, and nothing here treats it as such. The letter describes who is authorized to say a thing, never whether the thing is so.

Enabled is not a result

A source is enabled when collection is set up to visit it. Whether it was visited, returned nothing new, or could not be reached is a separate fact. Read its latest result on Coverage before interpreting an empty count or a quiet day.
