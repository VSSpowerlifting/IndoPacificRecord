Editorial team

The people who do the research, editorial review and communications behind the record.

Benjamin Yang

Founder & Executive Director

Benjamin Yang studies International Affairs at George Washington University's Elliott School, with a focus on Indo-Pacific security, U.S.–China relations, and regional strategic affairs. He founded Indo-Pacific Record to make official defense and security publications more accessible and to examine what those sources reveal—and leave unresolved—about regional developments. As Executive Director, he leads the publication's research and editorial direction, organizational operations, and platform development, emphasizing primary-source evidence, transparent documentation, and careful distinctions between official claims and independent analysis.

Dylan Tanzi

Lead Research Analyst

Dylan Tanzi studies International Politics at Georgetown University's Walsh School of Foreign Service. As Lead Research Analyst at Indo-Pacific Record, he has helped establish the Vietnam Research Desk and expand the publication's Southeast Asian coverage. His work focuses on primary-source research, translation verification, and editorial review of regional security and foreign policy analysis, with particular interests in Southeast Asian affairs, international security, and historical memory.

Jophy Lin

Communications Editor

Jophy Lin studies International Affairs at the George Washington University’s Elliott School of International Affairs, where he is pursuing a Bachelor of Science with a second major in Finance on a pre-law track.
