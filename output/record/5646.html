Summary

The Government Office transmitted Prime Minister instructions requiring the Ministry of Industry and Trade to review actual quantities and reserves of common construction material quarries across provinces, decide on inter-provincial coordination, and report to the Prime Minister by 25 October 2026. Provincial People's Committee Chairpersons were separately instructed to immediately audit quarries in their localities, revoke non-compliant licences, and report results by 30 October 2026.
