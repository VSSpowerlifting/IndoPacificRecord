China Military Online published this commemorative piece on the 75th anniversary of the CPVA's October 19, 1950 crossing of the Yalu River, weaving together the origins of three canonical Korean War songs with individual martyr stories — including Huang Jiguang, Li Zhengming of the 208th Regiment/70th Division/24th Army, and CPVA veteran Cheng Maoyou — and noting that thirteen repatriation batches have returned 1,023 sets of remains since 2014. The piece fits the recurring pattern of PLA political-work media using Korean War commemoration to reinforce the 伟大抗美援朝精神 (great spirit of the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea) as a living ideological resource, with the framing of technological inferiority overcome by sacrifice pointing toward present-day civil-military mobilization themes. Its value is as a record of how official military media constructs that ideological lineage for a contemporary audience, not as evidence of any operational or policy shift.
