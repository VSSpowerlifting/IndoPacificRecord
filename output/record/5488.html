The minister reviewed outcomes of the second Vietnam–Australia Ministerial Security Dialogue, co-chaired with Australian Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke in August 2026, which the report says produced agreed directions on border security, cybersecurity, transnational crime, peacekeeping deployment support, English-language training for MPS personnel, and technical and critical-technology cooperation.
