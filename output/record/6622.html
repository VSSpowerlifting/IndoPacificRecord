Chinese President Xi Jinping holds a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, Nov. 19, 2026. (Xinhua/Shen Hong) SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday chaired a historic summit here with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump, marking the first-ever face-to-face meeting between the Chinese, Russian and U.S. heads of state. At the summit, held in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, the three leaders had an in-depth exchange of views on the current international situation and committed to maintaining strategic stability and international security. Following the summit, the three countries released the China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward Peace, outlining their common understandings on issues including the UN reform, energy security, the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court and nuclear risk reduction. Xi described the trilateral meeting as being of groundbreaking significance. He said peace among major countries brings stability to the world, while their contention would only lead to chaos. Peace has been the enduring pursuit of humanity, Xi said, noting that the planet has experienced more than 10,000 wars and that more than 60 conflicts are still underway in various parts of the world. More than 80 years ago, China, Russia and the United States, the key anti-fascist forces in the world, fought shoulder to shoulder and achieved great victories, winning peace and tranquility for the world, he said. Chinese President Xi Jinping holds a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, Nov. 19, 2026. (Xinhua/Xie Huanchi) RIGHT WAY FOR MAJOR COUNTRIES TO GET ALONG "Humanity, living in the same global village, is a community with a shared future where we all stand to benefit or lose together," Xi said. "In this global village, major countries shoulder special responsibilities. Their positive interaction is not optional, but a must." In recent years, China has been promoting the China-Russia comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination for the new era and actively developing the constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability, with a view to fostering major-country dynamics featuring peaceful coexistence, overall stability and balanced development to the benefit of each other and the world at large, he said. Noting that transformation not seen in a century is accelerating across the globe, Xi said the world is not peaceful and testing times reveal responsibilities on one's shoulders. As major countries and permanent members of the UN Security Council, China, Russia and the United States should set good examples, work together to meet global challenges, jointly uphold world peace and stability, and make greater contributions to the progress of humanity, Xi said. Xi made four points on how major countries could find the right way to get along and jointly underpin world peace, calling for mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and dialogue and consultation. He said that countries should respect one another's sovereignty and territorial integrity, respect the social systems and development paths of their own choice, and respect each other's core interests and major concerns. They should not interfere in other countries' domestic affairs or each other's internal affairs, he said. "On major sensitive issues concerning one another, it is important to communicate in advance, try to see things from one another's perspective, act with caution, and in particular, not to challenge the red lines of one another," Xi said. Xi said that China, Russia and the United States should be partners and friends. "The Thucydides Trap is by no means a destiny." As the main victors of World War II, the three countries should steadfastly safeguard the postwar international order, uphold the purposes and principles of the UN Charter, and defend the hard-won outcomes of victory in World War II, Xi said. According to the trilateral declaration, the three heads of state reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought. The three countries will conduct a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons. "Nuclear weapons, the Sword of Damocles that hangs over humanity, should be completely prohibited and thoroughly destroyed over time to make the world free of nuclear weapons," Xi said. He emphasized the importance of win-win cooperation. The three countries should strengthen economic cooperation and trade, and combine their respective strengths in resources, manufacturing and consumption to make the pie of cooperation bigger and achieve win-win and all-win outcomes. "Decoupling and disruption of industrial and supply chains are not the way to go," Xi said, adding that the three countries could strengthen communication and coordination to safeguard the security and stability of the global energy market. He said that the three countries should maintain regular high-level exchanges, keep dialogue channels open at all levels, and establish crisis communication and prevention mechanisms to ensure that misjudgments occur less, differences can be managed, and frictions will not escalate. "China, Russia and the United States should continue to encourage parties to a conflict to sit down and talk, and settle disputes through political and diplomatic means," he said. Chinese President Xi Jinping holds a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, Nov. 19, 2026. (Xinhua/Yan Yan) POSITIVE, CONSTRUCTIVE AND FRUITFUL SUMMIT Putin said that the victory in World War II brought the three countries together. Safeguarding the purposes and principles of the UN Charter and upholding international peace are their shared responsibilities, while properly addressing hotspot issues serves their common interests, he added. Russia stands ready to work with China and the United States to promote free and open international trade, safeguard energy and food security, and strengthen international cooperation on artificial intelligence governance, Putin said. Trump said that the United States, China and Russia, as nuclear powers, bear a special and important responsibility for maintaining world peace and stability. Trump said the summit is a historic event of great importance, drawing global attention. He said the three countries should jointly prevent nuclear proliferation and avert the catastrophic consequences that nuclear weapons could bring to humanity. Noting that dialogue is essential to maintaining stability and achieving peace, Trump said all issues should be resolved through dialogue. He expressed hope that the three countries better protect the planet and make the future safer. The three heads of state also exchanged views on major international and regional issues, including the Ukraine crisis, nuclear non-proliferation, the situations in the Middle East and on the Korean Peninsula. Summarizing main consensus reached at the summit, Xi described the meeting as positive, constructive and fruitful, saying that the three countries share the same view on safeguarding world peace. Xi said that the three countries remember their history of fighting side by side during World War II and stressed the need to cherish the hard-won peace. Major countries should take seriously the legitimate security concerns of all nations, he added. Dialogue and negotiation represent the right way to resolve differences, Xi said. "There is hope for peace as long as dialogue does not cease and the door does not close." He stressed that cooperation is the only way to meet challenges, calling for stronger cooperation on the basis of common interests to advance peace and pursue win-win outcomes. "The seeds sown at today's summit will surely take root, sprout, blossom and bear fruit," Xi said, calling on the three countries to follow through on the consensus reached at the summit, strengthen communication, build common ground through dialogue, promote peace via cooperation, and work together to build a better world.
