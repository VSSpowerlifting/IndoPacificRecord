国防部：日本右翼势力种种行径是对战后国际秩序的公然挑衅 MND: Acts of Japanese Right-wing Forces Blatantly Challenge the Postwar International Order 11月13日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。 记者：据报道，日本新版“安保三文件”中拟继续将中国定位为“最大战略挑战”，未来五年防卫预算开支将提升至70万亿至80万亿日元。日本政府考虑修订《国家安保战略》时写入“无核三原则”例外情况，或允许在紧急情况下将核武器运进日本。请问对此有何评论？ Question:It's reported that Japan will continue to label China as its greatest strategic challenge in its revised three security documents, and increase its defense spending to 70 to 80 trillion yen over the next five years. The Japanese government is considering amending its National Security Strategy to include exceptions to the three non-nuclear principles, which potentially allows the transport of nuclear weapons into Japan in emergencies. May I have your comment on that? 陈曦：日本是二战战败国。历史上日本军国主义曾经犯下滔天罪行，给亚太和世界人民造成深重灾难。日方本应深刻反省侵略历史和所犯罪责，深刻汲取历史教训。然而日本右翼势力却不断编造所谓“中国威胁论”的虚假叙事，黑白颠倒编排“受害者”戏码，加速扩充防卫开支，强化进攻性作战和远程攻击力量，现在更是妄图实现渐进式核松绑，种种行径不仅是对自身和平承诺的背叛，更是对战后国际秩序和《不扩散核武器条约》权威性的公然挑衅。国际社会应高度警惕、共同遏止日本“再军事化”妄动，维护国际核不扩散体系，捍卫国际安全与和平。 Chen Xi:Japan is a defeated country in WWII. Looking back, Japanese militarists committed heinous crimes, inflicting untold suffering on people in the Asia-Pacific and beyond. The Japanese side should have reflected on its history of aggression and war crimes, and drawn lessons from history. Instead, right-wing forces in Japan keep fabricating false narratives on the so-called China threat, and distorting facts to portray Japan as a victim. At the same time, they are ramping up defense expenditure, strengthening offensive and long-range strike capabilities, and, worse still, seeking to progressively ease nuclear restrictions. With such moves, they have not only betrayed Japan's commitment to peace, but also blatantly challenged the postwar international order and the authority of the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons. The international community needs to stay highly vigilant and take countermeasures against Japan's push for remilitarization, uphold the international nuclear non-proliferation regime, and defend security and peace of the world.
