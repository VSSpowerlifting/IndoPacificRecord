Scope and coverage

This snapshot contains public records from China Desk, Singapore Desk, South Korea Desk, Vietnam Desk. Coverage is selective and concentrated in Chinese sources. Other desks contribute no public records to this snapshot; their scope and limitations are described in coverage detail.
