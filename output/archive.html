    Search scope and counts

    Title contains keeps literal title matching. Keywords require every term or quoted phrase. All published material adds public official-link references, registered originals, reviewed English artifacts and human-controlled analysis. Archive dates remain source-stated. IPR publication dates come from public release records; unrecorded dates stay unrecorded. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Processing state, institution, original language and source-trail filters apply to archive records. Search counts are documents, not events or independent corroboration.

    Archive title search covers titles only — the English title where one exists, and the original title always. It does not search machine summaries or stored source text.

    Source outlets and publishing institutions overlap: one institution can publish through more than one source, so their counts are not mutually exclusive categories. Original language is an attribute of the source, not something asserted independently for each record. Counts are stored, deduplicated records, not an institution's output.
