      How search and counts work

      Title contains matches the English title where one exists and the original title always. It does not search machine summaries or stored source text. Keywords, under More filters, must all appear; put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly. They also read the names of the source, institution and desk. Dates are the publication dates each source stated.

      All published material adds IPR Briefs, published originals, reviewed English texts and links to official pages, and there keywords also read the summaries and texts IPR publishes. The processing status, institution, language and cited-in-analysis filters apply only to source records.

      Counts are stored records with duplicates removed: not events, not independent corroboration and not an institution's total output. Sources and institutions overlap: one institution can publish through more than one source, so their counts are not mutually exclusive categories. Original language is an attribute of the source, not something asserted independently for each record.
