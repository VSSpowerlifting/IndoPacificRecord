    How search and counts work

    Title contains matches the English title where one exists and the original title always. It does not search machine summaries or stored source text. Keywords must all appear; put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD and are the dates the source stated.

    All published material adds IPR Briefs, registered originals, reviewed English texts and links to official pages. The status, institution, language and analysis filters apply only to source records.

    Counts are stored, deduplicated records: not events, not independent corroboration and not an institution's total output. Sources and institutions overlap: one institution can publish through more than one source, so their counts are not mutually exclusive categories. Original language is an attribute of the source, not something asserted independently for each record.
